8U Practice

Zero-idle-time generator.

Total Time 59:59
Station Timer 07:59

Outdoor • 2 Coaches

1. Dynamic Warmup 8 MIN

Follow the Leader Sprints

Setup: Single file line behind coach on foul line.

Execution: Coach jogs, shuffles, and sprints. Kids must mirror exact movements. Whistle means freeze.

Culture Cue: "Eyes on me." Don't start speaking until every kid is actively listening. Praise the first kid to freeze on the whistle.

3. Station Rotations 30 MIN
Station 1 • Coach Steve

Two-Line Rapid Fire

Split team into two parallel lines (SS and 2B). Both coaches stand near home plate. Hit grounders rapidly alternating lines. Fielder throws to target net and hustles to back of opposite line.

Culture Cue: Hustle standard. Praise the kids who sprint to the back of the opposite line, not just the ones who make a good throw.

Station 2 • Coach Assistant

Heavy Ball Front Toss

L-screen 15 feet from home plate. Batter in box, one on deck, rest behind fence. Feeder tosses weighted balls to front hip. Rotate every 10 swings.
